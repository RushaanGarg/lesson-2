Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi's full birth name is Mohandas karamchand Gandhi. The word "mahatma"" means "great soul" and is an honorary title given to him later in life, rather than a part of his given birth name.

One of his great works

The Dandi march was a 24-day nonviolent protest led by Mahatma Gandhi against the British salt monopoly in colonial India, starting on March 12, 1930, and ending on April 6, 1930. It spanned 387 kilometers (240 miles) from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi.
